Province Overview

Located in the eastern coast of Mindanao, Surigao del Sur features diverse landscapes, from mountain ranges to coastal plains. It is part of the Caraga Region and is rich in biodiversity.

Best Time to Visit

The dry months from March to May are ideal for island hopping and waterfall tours, while the rest of the year offers lush scenery and cooler conditions in upland areas.

How to Get There

Travelers usually arrive via Butuan or Davao and continue by bus or van to major towns such as Tandag, Bislig, and Hinatuan.
